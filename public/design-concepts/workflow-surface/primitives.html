Primitives reference

Every workflow-shaped surface in Spectre composes from the nine primitives on this page. New workflow types add configuration, not primitives. This page is the visual contract every implementation must respect.

03Approval bar

Always renders every workflow-appropriate action. The primary button is the workflow's default approval verb. Secondary actions are always visible — the human is one click from disagreeing with Spectre.

04Evidence panel

Renders the raw material the AI reasoned over. Per-section collapse; collapsed by default when overall confidence is high. Every extracted field carries per-field confidence where applicable. Six section kinds: document, extracted, match, context, preview, history.

05Activity timeline

The primitive that enforces AI transparency. Every event — human or AI — appends a row with actor, verb, timestamp, and reason. Append-only; reverts do not delete rows, they add new ones. This is what auditors read.

06Conversation thread

Human-to-human (or human-to-AI) messaging when the operator needs clarification. Three recipient kinds: external (vendor / applicant), internal (another Spectre user), Spectre (asks the AI to re-run enrichment with a hint). Every message writes to the timeline.

07Workflow banner

Compact top-of-detail banners for one-shot state announcements. Composes with the Data Workspace inspector banner styles (ok / warn / err). Persistent for exception and hold; transient for the rest.

08Workflow queue

A Data Workspace consumer for one workflow type. Inherits every Data Workspace primitive (search, saved views, density, selection, keyboard model). Grouped by AI recommendation strength, not by state. See the standalone queue.html concept.

09Workflow detail

The single-item surface. Two columns: Evidence Panel (left, dominant) + Workflow Rail (right, stable). Evidence dominates because it is what the human is judging. See the standalone detail.html concept.

·Forbidden by the primitive contract

The primitives enforce these anti-patterns at the type level:

  • ❌ RecommendationCard without reasoning or confidence
  • ❌ ApprovalBar without every workflow-appropriate action visible
  • ❌ ActivityTimeline missing an entry for any state transition
  • ❌ StateChip conveying state by colour alone
  • ❌ AI actions writing to a domain row without a timeline row
  • ❌ "Recommend approve" tone when confidence is below the workflow's threshold
  • ❌ Irreversible action without a declared revert window (or explicit founder exemption)
  • ❌ Modal-only detail views
  • ❌ Bulk actions that hide per-item results
  • ❌ Autosave on approval fields
