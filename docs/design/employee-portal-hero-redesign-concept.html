What the proposed hero encodes

Structure

  • Hero begins at the top of the content column — no top chrome bar.
  • Club name upper-left inside the hero (serif, not the current SaaS-style brand bar).
  • Employee photo + full name + employee number upper-right, inside the hero.
  • Greeting (time-of-day) lower-left, serif, dominant.
  • Current position sits beneath the greeting as a subtle second line.
  • Left rail remains: Employee Portal → Home → Profile.

What stays out

  • No brand chrome at the very top of the viewport.
  • No Spectre wordmark anywhere — the club is the brand.
  • No decorative badges, chips, or verdict pills in the hero.
  • Widget grid below the hero is unchanged from today.
  • Tour anchoring stays keyed to widget tour targets (drawer↔tour isolation from Commit A is preserved).

Why it works

  • Removes a redundant identity strip — the top chrome and the hero card were saying the same thing twice.
  • Puts the greeting where the eye lands first on any device — hero owns the fold.
  • Signals "your Club, not Spectre" via the serif Club name in the hero corner.
  • Keeps the current mobile widget compaction from Commit A intact.

Open questions for the founder

  • Photo shape: circular (as drawn) or squared-off with a subtle radius?
  • Employee number display: prefix-with-# (as drawn) or spelled as "Employee 0129"?
  • Notifications bell: outside the hero, inside the hero corner, or on the Home canvas?
  • Hero background: solid cream, subtle gradient (as drawn), or per-Club branded image (opens the branding story)?
